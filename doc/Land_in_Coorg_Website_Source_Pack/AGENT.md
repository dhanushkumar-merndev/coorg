## 2. Non-negotiable source handling rules

1. Do not assume that a number such as `₹45 lakh`, `₹55 lakh`, or `₹60 lakh` means a per-acre rate. The client did not state the basis.
2. Do not convert the raw note `50 caree` to `50 acres` publicly until confirmed.
3. Do not attach a forest/waterfall/drone asset to a specific acreage listing unless the client confirms the association.
4. Do not describe a waterfall as private, on-site, inside the property, or adjoining the property without confirmation.
5. Do not describe rendered/AI imagery as completed real-world infrastructure.
6. ABBELON 3D plans are marketing/illustrative material. Legal dimensions, numbering, approvals, and sanctioned layout must come from the original approved plan.
7. Do not publish crop-revenue or investment-return claims for SLN until the client resolves contradictory figures in the source PDF.
8. The Hoodi property is in Bangalore and is **out of the default Land in Coorg scope**. Do not mix it into Coorg inventory unless the client explicitly expands the website scope.
9. The mobile number for Computer Raju belongs to the 20-acre Basavanahalli lead in the source notes. Do not treat it as the general Land in Coorg website contact unless the client confirms.
10. Keep missing data visibly unresolved in the internal content model (TBC / Not supplied). Do not fill it with general knowledge.
